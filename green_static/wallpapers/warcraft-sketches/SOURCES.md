# Warcraft sketch collection

Original images downloaded 2026-09-29; no recoloring, cropping, or AI alterations.
Artwork belongs to Blizzard Entertainment and its artists. Archive locations:

- `01-wow-building-studies.jpg`: [Vanilla WoW gallery](https://archivecraft.com/archives/artwork/vanilla/), image 154, structures and Booty Bay studies.
- `02-wow-bridge-studies.jpg`: same gallery, image 157.
- `03-wow-tower-studies.jpg`: same gallery, image 170.
- `04-warcraft3-mortar-team.jpg`: [Warcraft III concept art / original press-kit gallery](https://www.mobygames.com/game/6860/warcraft-iii-reign-of-chaos/promo/group-179506/image-1418462/), Samwise Didier, 1999; 827 × 600.
- `05-warcraft2-kilrogg.jpg`: [JudgeHype image archive](https://warcraft.judgehype.com/image/778868/). The illustration is identified as Warcraft II expansion manual art on [Samwise Didier's own page](https://www.deviantart.com/samwisedidier/art/Warcraft-Killrog-WarCraft-II-Exp-Manual-450475810); the archive's gallery groups it with earlier Warcraft art.
